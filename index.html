I am an Instructor in Mathematics at Princeton University. In Spring 2024, I was a postdoctoral fellow at SLMath (formerly MSRI), and in Fall 2023, I was a member of the Institute for Advanced Study. Before that, I was a graduate student at Cornell University, where I was advised by Daniel Halpern-Leistner.

My research is in algebraic geometry, especially derived categories and their connections with birational geometry. I am also interested in geometric invariant theory, noncommutative geometry, and moduli theory.

Publications and preprints

A looming of phantoms

With D. Krashen, T. Liu, Y. Liu, E. Mackall, S. Makarova, A. Perry, A.-A. Robotis, and S. Venkatesh.

Advances in Mathematics 499 (2026), article 111046.

arXiv ↗ Journal ↗
− Hide description+ Show description

We construct new examples of phantom categories in the derived categories of two kinds of rational surfaces: the blowup of the plane at 11 points in general position, and the blowup of the second Hirzebruch surface at nine points in general position. We also propose conjectures for blowups of other Hirzebruch surfaces.

The Fano of lines, the Kuznetsov component, and a flop

With E. Segal.

Preprint (2025).

arXiv ↗
− Hide description+ Show description

We prove Galkin's conjecture for cubic fourfolds: the symmetric square of the Kuznetsov component is equivalent to the derived category of the Fano variety of lines. A key ingredient is a derived equivalence for a 12-dimensional flop.

Non-existence of phantoms on some non-generic blowups of the projective plane

With L. Borisov.

Proceedings of the American Mathematical Society 153 (2025), no. 3, 963–968.

arXiv ↗ Journal ↗
− Hide description+ Show description

We show that the derived category of the blowup of the projective plane at points in very general position on a smooth cubic curve contains no phantom subcategories. We also propose a conjectural obstruction to phantoms on surfaces.

Full exceptional collections of vector bundles on rank-two linear GIT quotients

With D. Halpern-Leistner.

Advances in Mathematics 482 (2025), article 110638.

arXiv ↗ Journal ↗
− Hide description+ Show description

Using window categories, we construct full strong exceptional collections of vector bundles on certain linear GIT quotients by reductive groups of rank two. We also obtain further examples among decorated quiver varieties.
